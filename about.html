Our story

Africa's energy gap is a business problem first

Across Nigeria's busiest SME clusters — from Ikotun Market in Lagos to trading hubs planned for Onitsha, Aba and beyond — the grid is treated less like infrastructure and more like a rumour. Business owners build fuel costs, spoilage risk and lost trading hours into every plan, because they have to.

SteadFlow Energies started with a simple question: what if solar power for a small business was as easy to get, finance and understand as a mobile phone plan? That question shaped everything that followed — from how we size systems, to how SteadFlow IQ explains a battery reading in plain language, to why we built a marketplace instead of asking customers to source parts themselves.
